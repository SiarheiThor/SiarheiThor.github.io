About Me

I’m an ML engineer based in Zurich. I build data products end to end: from scattered raw data to a dataset, models and a service that people use, with evaluation that holds up in production.

I studied architecture at TU Delft and worked 7 years as an architect (competitions, BIM, computational design), writing code since 2014. Then I ran my own architecture, urban planning and AI consulting practice for two years. For the last three years I’ve built production ML: regression models on time-series sensor data and LLM systems.

What I’ve built

  • Quevita: turned scattered athlete training files into a population-level dataset that drives athlete insights, performance prediction and training advice; I own the prediction platform built on it
  • An agentic pipeline that keeps a race database current, replacing manual curation
  • Perspetivo: conversational analytics over survey data, with statistical guardrails against findings from samples too small to trust
  • STHLM22: won the Moscow “Renovation” urban planning competition (about 2 million m²); RAG assistant for building regulations
  • Telegram-based AI products for learning, productivity and tracking

Research

On the side, I research AI for design:

  • Controllability: steering generative systems toward what the designer intends
  • Design novelty: whether AI can come up with new solutions or only recombine what it has seen
  • Self-improvement: systems that evaluate their own output and improve from it

Stack

Python, FastAPI, XGBoost, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, pandas, SQL/PostgreSQL, Docker, GCP, LLM agents and RAG, Rhino/Grasshopper
