About Me

I’m an architect and AI engineer based in Zurich.

I studied architecture at TU Delft and worked in practice for 7 years (competitions, BIM, computational design). Then I moved into AI and have spent 5 years in data science and ML engineering, building production systems.

Research

I’m currently researching AI for design:

  • Controllability: steering generative systems toward what the designer intends
  • Design novelty: whether AI can come up with new solutions or only recombine what it has seen
  • Self-improvement: systems that evaluate their own output and improve from it

What I’ve built

  • Production ML services for endurance-sports analytics
  • AI-assisted data enrichment workflows
  • Conversational analytics over survey data
  • Retrieval (RAG) for building regulations (STHLM22)
  • Telegram-based AI products for learning, productivity and tracking

Stack

Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, scikit-learn, XGBoost, pandas, SQL, Docker, LLM tooling (retrieval, agents, evaluation)
